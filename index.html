About Us

Our Story

Since the first bloom

Flowers That Speak from the Heart

Bloom & Bliss began with a simple belief: every bouquet carries a story — a first date, a milestone, a thank-you, a goodbye. We started as a tiny home studio, hand-tying arrangements for friends and family, and grew into a florist devoted to turning feelings into flowers.

Our mission is to make thoughtful, beautiful floral gifting effortless — with fresh blooms, honest prices, and the personal touches that make each order feel one-of-a-kind.

Our promise: farm-fresh flowers, careful hand-arranging, on-time delivery, and a smile with every order.
